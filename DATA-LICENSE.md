The OpenAutoAC database is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

The full license text is available in the `LICENSE` file of this repository.

## Database contents

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the individual factual contents contributed to OpenAutoAC are made available under the Database Contents License (DbCL) 1.0.

This includes factual automotive air-conditioning data such as:

* vehicle manufacturer;
* model;
* generation;
* production years;
* engine or variant information;
* refrigerant type;
* refrigerant charge quantity;
* refrigerant charge tolerance;
* compressor oil specification;
* compressor oil quantity;
* compressor type.

## Third-party material

Third-party documents, photographs, websites, manuals, manufacturer documentation and commercial databases referenced as sources are **not automatically covered by the OpenAutoAC license**.

OpenAutoAC records factual information and provenance references but does not grant rights over third-party source material.

## Source requirements

Every contributed record must have a documented and verifiable source.

Data must not be copied, extracted, scraped or systematically reconstructed from proprietary or commercial databases unless the contributor can demonstrate that redistribution under the OpenAutoAC license is explicitly authorized.

Examples of restricted sources may include:

* Autodata;
* TecRMI / TecAlliance;
* HaynesPro;
* MAHLE;
* HELLA;
* proprietary manufacturer databases;
* other commercial technical-information services.

Public accessibility of information does not automatically mean that the information or database may be reproduced or redistributed.

## Contributor declaration

By contributing data to OpenAutoAC, the contributor confirms that:

1. they have the right to submit the contributed information;
2. the contribution may legally be redistributed under the OpenAutoAC licensing terms;
3. the declared source is accurate;
4. the data was not obtained through unauthorized extraction from a protected database.

OpenAutoAC maintainers reserve the right to reject, quarantine or remove any contribution where provenance or redistribution rights cannot be reasonably verified.
